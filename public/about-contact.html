Our Story

Small batches, real ingredients, honest craft.

SoDak Sweet Tooth started right in Sioux Falls, South Dakota with a single kettle, a marble slab, and an uncompromising obsession with making sweets that taste like they came from a grandmother's kitchen.

Unlike industrial confectionery brands that sit in warehouses for months, every batch of our fudge, hand-rolled truffles, and chewy gummies is made fresh to order each week. We use pure dairy butter, heavy cream, and real fruit extracts — no artificial shortcuts.

Our Values

What guides everything we make

We believe small-batch candy should be fresh, thoughtful, and joyful.

No Shortcuts

We cook our caramel slowly and temper our chocolate by hand to get the exact texture and shine that mass production can never match.

Small Batch Freshness

Trays are made weekly. From our cooling tables to your door in days, ensuring maximum flavor, snap, and chew.

Delightful Gifting

Every box is packed with care, tissue, and protective mailers so receiving our sweets feels like opening a cherished gift.
